PATIENT CARE GUIDELINES

Inpatient Visiting Hours & Regulations

To ensure a quiet, therapeutic healing environment and control infection, visiting hours are strictly monitored.

Hospital Ward Category Morning Visiting Window Evening Visiting Window Max Visitors Allowed Pass Requirement
General Medical & Surgical Wards 11:00 AM – 12:30 PM 04:30 PM – 07:00 PM 2 Visitors at a time Standard Visitor Pass
Intensive Care Unit (ICU / CCU) 11:00 AM – 11:30 AM 05:00 PM – 05:30 PM 1 Immediate Family Member Special ICU Authorization

🛡️ Infection Control & Visitor Etiquette Rules:

  • Hand sanitization is mandatory at all ward entrances and bedside dispensers.
  • Children below 12 years are not permitted in intensive care zones for safety and health reasons.
  • Maintain low voice levels to respect patient rest and recovery. Mobile phones must be kept on silent.
